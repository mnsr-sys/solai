# SolAI solar carbon MRV methodology framework — v0.2

## Purpose

SolAI is designed to measure, report and verify renewable electricity generation and quantify potential avoided grid emissions for solar projects in Telangana, including projects located at, adjacent to, or electrically connected through substations.

A substation is an infrastructure and grid-context anchor. It does not itself create carbon reductions. The climate impact is attributable to eligible renewable electricity generated by a defined project and supplied to the grid or used in a way recognised by the applicable methodology.

This framework is a product design and pre-screening framework, not an approved carbon-credit methodology and not a guarantee of credit issuance.

## MRV workflow

1. **Project registration:** project ID, owner/rights holder, coordinates, technology, capacity, commissioning date, project boundary, grid connection and applicable permits.
2. **Methodology selection:** identify the applicable carbon-crediting programme and methodology before finalising monitoring parameters or making credit claims.
3. **Baseline definition:** document the counterfactual electricity source and the methodology-prescribed grid emission factor, vintage, geography and calculation approach.
4. **Monitoring:** collect calibrated generation-meter and, where relevant, export-meter readings; preserve interval, timezone, meter ID, units and source.
5. **Data QA/QC:** check missing intervals, duplicates, resets, outliers, meter replacement, calibration, outages, curtailment and reconciliation between generation, auxiliary consumption, export and self-consumption.
6. **Net eligible electricity:** calculate only the energy quantity recognised by the selected methodology and project boundary.
7. **Emissions calculation:** apply the methodology's baseline, project-emissions and leakage rules. Do not assume a generic grid factor is acceptable.
8. **Evidence and verification:** package raw meter records, calibration certificates, utility statements, commissioning documents, ownership/rights, calculations, monitoring reports and audit trail for independent validation/verification.

## Core energy data model

At minimum retain:
- project and asset identifiers;
- plant coordinates and project boundary;
- installed DC/AC capacity and commissioning date;
- inverter, generation meter and export meter identifiers;
- meter calibration and replacement history;
- interval timestamp, timezone, energy value, unit and source;
- gross generation, auxiliary consumption, grid export and on-site consumption where separately metered;
- outages, curtailment and data gaps;
- utility interconnection and energy accounting records;
- source file hash, ingestion timestamp, transformation version and reviewer status.

Never infer actual generation from installed capacity when metered generation is available. Modelled yield may be used for planning or gap estimation only when clearly labelled and permitted by the methodology.

## Emissions accounting

Conceptual expression only:

- Baseline emissions = eligible electricity quantity × methodology-prescribed baseline emission factor.
- Project emissions and leakage = calculated as required by the selected methodology.
- Net emission reductions = baseline emissions − project emissions − leakage, subject to methodology rules and applicable adjustments.

The eligible electricity quantity, emission factor, grid boundary, losses, temporal treatment and any adjustment must come from the selected methodology and authoritative data. A generic multiplication of annual solar generation by a grid factor is an indicative avoided-emissions estimate, not a carbon-credit calculation.

## Carbon-credit eligibility gates

Before describing a project as credit-eligible, assess:
- applicable programme and methodology status;
- project start date, commissioning date and registration deadlines;
- additionality and regulatory surplus where required;
- baseline scenario and grid boundary;
- ownership of environmental attributes and carbon rights;
- prior claims, renewable energy certificates and double counting/double issuance risks;
- monitoring plan and calibrated meters;
- leakage, project emissions and safeguards where relevant;
- stakeholder consultation and sustainable-development requirements where applicable;
- independent validation, verification and registry issuance.

## Status vocabulary

Use separate statuses:
- **Mapped asset:** location found in a public or supplied dataset.
- **Project registered:** project details supplied, not necessarily verified.
- **Meter data connected:** data ingestion is active; accuracy still subject to QA/QC.
- **Generation measured:** meter readings passed stated data checks.
- **Avoided emissions estimated:** indicative calculation, not verified reductions.
- **Methodology screened:** preliminary review against a named methodology.
- **Validation/verification pending:** submitted to an independent validation/verification body.
- **Verified reductions:** confirmed through the relevant independent process.
- **Credits issued:** registry has issued serialised credits.

Do not collapse these statuses into a single “carbon credits generated” label.

## Auditability

Retain raw source files, hashes, ingestion logs, meter metadata, transformations, calculation versions, emission-factor references, assumptions, exceptions, approvals and report versions. A cryptographic hash demonstrates file integrity after capture; it does not prove that the source meter, record or claim is true.

## Limitations

SolAI does not replace the project developer, utility, accredited validation/verification body or carbon-credit registry. Public mapping cannot prove ownership, project operation, metered generation, grid export, additionality or credit issuance.
